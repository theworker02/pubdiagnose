# PubDoctor Branding

**Product name:** PubDoctor  
**Package / CLI:** `pubdoctor`  
**Tagline:** Diagnose your Dart dependencies.

## Logo concept

Dependency nodes connected inside a package/cube silhouette, with a diagnostic check/pulse accent. Geometry is Dart-inspired (clean facets, teal depth) but **does not** copy the official Dart logo. Avoid medical clip-art.

## Clear space & minimum size

- Clear space around the mark: ≥ 1/8 of the mark width on all sides.
- Minimum digital size: **24px** (mark), **120px** wide (horizontal).
- Do not place the mark on busy photography without a solid scrim.

## Light / dark

- Prefer `logo-dark.svg` on dark UI and `logo-light.svg` / horizontal on light UI.
- Maintain contrast: body text on brand-deep or ink-dark should use cloud (`#E8F1F2`) or white.
- Accent (`#F4A261`) on deep teal meets UI accent needs; do not use accent for long body text.

## Typography

- Product UI / site: **Source Sans 3** or **IBM Plex Sans** (expressive, not Inter/Roboto/Arial defaults in marketing).
- Code / terminal samples: **IBM Plex Mono** or **JetBrains Mono**.
- Wordmark in SVG assets uses a clean grotesque for portability.

## Incorrect usage

- Do not recolor the mark into purple-glow AI clichés.
- Do not add medical crosses, stethoscopes, or caduceus imagery.
- Do not stretch, rotate beyond 15°, or add drop-shadow stacks.
- Do not place competing badges over the mark.
- Do not imply endorsement by the Dart or Flutter trademarks.

## Accessibility

- Text on `brand-deep` / `ink-dark` uses `#E8F1F2` (≥ 7:1 for large text).
- Interactive accents should not rely on color alone; pair with icons/labels.
- Social preview text is large and high-contrast for small-thumbnail readability.
